Executive profile

Dr. Bibek Malakar is a consultant psychiatrist and dedicated mental health advocate serving the Kathmandu and Chitwan regions of Nepal.

He is known for bridging advanced psychiatric science with deep cultural understanding. His work rests on three commitments: compassionate, evidence-based clinical care, wider public awareness, and reducing the stigma surrounding mental illness across the country.

Clinical care

Inpatient and outpatient psychiatry with modern treatments and structured therapy.

Public awareness

Educational content on sleep, grief, substance use and everyday coping.

Less stigma

Training community health volunteers to spot early signs of mental illness.
